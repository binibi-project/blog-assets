How K-Pop Concert Tickets Actually Work in Korea

Fan club presale, the new Korea-first global-second split, and the law that just made scalping a lot more expensive.

Quick answer
  • Fan club membership is the real ticket: for a group this size, general sale is mostly what fan club members didn't take.
  • Korea now goes first: since Stray Kids' "Run It" tour, domestic fans book a full day before fans abroad, on a platform split into separate local and global pages.
  • Reselling just got expensive: a decree that took effect August 28, 2026 fines scalpers up to 50 times what they charged.

Getting into a Stray Kids show has surprisingly little to do with luck. It looks like luck from the outside: a countdown clock, a refresh button, a queue number that either loads in time or doesn't. But almost everyone who gets a seat got there the same boring way, weeks earlier, by joining a fan club and reading a notice page closely enough to know which button to click and when. The scramble everyone talks about is really the last five minutes of a process that started a month before.

What is a "fan club presale" (팬클럽 선예매)?

A block of tickets an agency sets aside for verified fan club members, sold before anyone else can buy. For a popular act, this is most of the good inventory; whatever's left goes to general sale.

🎟️ Is a fan club membership actually required?

Not required, but close to it in practice. Stray Kids' agency, JYP Entertainment, ran STAY 6th-generation membership recruitment from January 26 to February 4, 2026 through its FANS platform, at a fee of ₩39,900 (about $30 at ₩1,336/$1). That membership is what got someone into the presale for the "Run It" world tour's Seoul run: five shows at KSPO Dome on July 25, 26, 29, and August 1-2, ticketed at a flat ₩154,000 (about $115).

The presale itself ran in three stages: Korea's domestic booking page opened Monday, June 29 at 8 p.m.; the global page followed Tuesday, June 30 at 8 p.m.; general sale opened to everyone Wednesday, July 1 at 8 p.m.. Fan club members got one ticket per person per show in presale; general sale allowed two per person per show, presale tickets included.

Set up your account correctly the first time. NOL World, the global booking page, requires verification with a non-Korean passport, and admission only works when that ID matches the ticket holder exactly. One queue slot is allowed per account, and opening a second device closes the first.

💳 Why does joining a fan club cost money at all?

Because presale access is the one benefit fans actually want, and agencies know it. STAY's ₩39,900 fee originally didn't include the membership kit; JYP priced that separately at ₩22,000, and after fan backlash over paying more for less, cut it to ₩10,000, which still pushes the total toward ₩50,000. Other agencies charge less: HYBE's fan clubs run about ₩25,000 plus a ₩15,000 kit, and SM Entertainment's about ₩30,000 plus ₩8,800.

Concert tickets sit on top of that. Stray Kids' own "dominATE: celebrATE" show at Incheon Asiad Main Stadium priced its floor and center SR seats at ₩165,000 (about $124), which is actually on the cheaper end: Enhypen's "Walk the Line: Final" charged ₩220,000 (about $165) for standing floor seats, and NCT Dream's "THE DREAM SHOW 4" priced VIP seats at ₩198,000 (about $148).

What's in a "membership kit" (멤버십 키트)?

A small box sold to fan club members, usually a photobook, photocards, and similar merchandise. It used to come bundled into the membership fee; several groups now sell it separately, which is part of why total costs are climbing.

🌍 Why does Korea get to book first, and what stops reselling now?

Because JYP split fan club presale into two rounds instead of one, and the law now punishes scalpers hard. Before "Run It," STAY members worldwide could book the moment presale opened, wherever they lived. Starting with this tour, Korea-based fans book Monday and fans abroad book Tuesday, with the domestic page requiring Korean ID and phone verification that fans overseas generally can't provide.

Stray Kids isn't the only case. Enhypen introduced a similar Korea-first presale in May for "Blood Saga," and Cortis, Le Sserafim, and SM acts including Red Velvet, NCT Dream, and aespa have since followed. One international fan summed up the frustration this way: "International fans don't have the same opportunities to see Stray Kids like us, fans who live in Korea, have. They're already at a disadvantage."

Reselling above face value now risks a fine up to 50 times what was charged. Revised enforcement decrees under the Performing Arts Act and National Sports Promotion Act took effect August 28, 2026, letting authorities fine illegal resellers anywhere from 2 to 50 times the sale amount, scaled to how many tickets and how much money were involved. Criminal penalties run up to one year in prison or a ₩10 million fine on top of that.

The rule applies whether or not a scalper used bot software to buy in bulk, and it covers both the reseller and, for illegal bulk buying, the buyer. It kicks in once a sale is ₩50,000 or more and involves at least two tickets for a performance, or ₩20,000 and two tickets for a sports event. Report an illegal purchase and the reward runs up to ₩50 million; report an illegal sale and it's up to half of whatever fine gets imposed on the seller. Movie tickets are explicitly left out of this round of rules.

Check before you go
  • Join the fan club before the recruitment window closes: STAY's 6th-gen sign-up ran a fixed nine days; miss it and there's no presale until the next round opens.
  • Verify with a passport, not a Korean ID, if you live outside Korea: mixing the two on NOL World locks you out of your own queue.
  • Skip resale listings above face value after August 28, 2026: both sides of that trade can now be fined.
My take I don't follow a specific group closely enough to have gone through a fan club presale myself, so I can't tell you what it feels like to watch that queue number tick down. But I do most of my own shopping and bill-paying from a phone in Seoul, and the math here isn't complicated: if a show is worth crossing an ocean or a city for, the ₩39,900 fee is cheaper than the flight, and general sale for a group this size is not a plan. I'd pay for the membership the day it opens and treat presale as the only real shot.
Go deeper
  • If you have one more day before a presale spend it finishing identity verification on the correct page, domestic or global, since most platforms lock you into whichever one you verified on first.
  • What locals argue about whether fan clubs still function as a fan community or have become, in one commenter's words, "a toll booth for buying tickets" now that presale is their main draw.
  • Watch for each tour's own notice page: presale order, verification cutoff times, and ticket limits are set per tour, not fixed rules.
FAQ
Can I buy Stray Kids tickets without joining the fan club?
Technically yes, through general sale, but for a tour like "Run It" that sale opens after two rounds of fan club presale have already taken the best seats, and often much of the inventory.
Is NOL World safe to use without a Korean phone number?
Yes, that's exactly the platform it's built for: verification runs on a passport instead of a Korean ID, and support is reachable by email for account issues.

Membership gets a fan into the room where tickets are decided; it doesn't guarantee a seat, and the room itself now has a Korea line and a global line inside it. My next check-in on this is whenever STAY's 7th generation opens, to see whether the domestic-global gap holds for a tour announced further in advance.
