Nami Island Day Trip from Seoul: What It Actually Costs

The 06:17 ITX to Gapyeong, the ₩19,000 round-trip ferry, and when the metasequoia avenue and ginkgo path actually turn color this fall.

Quick answer
  • ₩19,000 covers the whole crossing: admission and both ferry rides over and back, for one adult.
  • Take the 06:17 ITX from Cheongnyangni: 40 minutes to Gapyeong for ₩6,900, versus ₩2,300 and about 67 minutes on the slower commuter train.
  • Buy before 8 a.m.: the early-bird rate drops the same ticket from ₩19,000 to ₩13,000.

The gate attendant stamps a little passport-style ticket before you even reach the boat, because Nami Island has spent decades insisting it isn't really part of Korea at all. Out on the water the ferry's canopy is strung with a dozen flags that belong to no real country, and by the time you step off on the other side the trees have already closed in: a straight corridor of trunks running off toward a vanishing point, quiet enough that you can hear your own shoes on the gravel. It's the kind of quiet that doesn't happen by accident on a Saturday near Seoul, and it's the reason a man-made river island keeps pulling people back every autumn.

If you've seen a drama scene where two people walk a tree-lined path in the snow and say almost nothing, there's a decent chance it was filmed right here.

🚆 How do you actually get from Seoul to Nami Island?

The fastest way is the ITX-Cheongchun train from Cheongnyangni Station to Gapyeong, which runs about 40 minutes and costs ₩6,900 one-way for an adult in general seating. Korail's own fare table lists that exact figure, along with the first departure toward Gapyeong at 06:17 and a last train back from Gapyeong to Cheongnyangni at around 22:31.

If ₩6,900 feels steep for a 40-minute ride, the regular Gyeongchun Line commuter train covers the same stretch for ₩2,300 with a T-money card, just slower at around 67 minutes and with more stops along the way. Either train lands you at Gapyeong Station, which sits about 1.5 to 2 km from the ferry pier itself.

What is the "Naminara Republic"?

Nami Island's own name for itself: a tourist attraction dressed up as a tiny imaginary nation, complete with its own entry stamp and flag-draped ferries. It isn't a real country, but the bit is consistent enough that the "passport" ticket is part of the fun.

From there, a taxi waiting right outside the station gets you to the pier in about 5 minutes for close to the base fare, which is the easier call if you're hauling a bag or running close to a train connection. The walk covers the same ground along a flat road, if you'd rather save the fare and stretch your legs after the train.

💸 What does a full day actually cost?

A single adult can do the island for ₩19,000, and that ticket already includes both ferry crossings. The Chuncheon city tourism office lists the same general admission at ₩19,000, a discounted ₩16,000 for middle and high schoolers and seniors 70 and older, and ₩13,000 for children from 36 months through elementary age.

On paper the zipwire looks like a separate attraction, but both courses already fold in the same island admission and a boat ride back, so the extra ₩30,000-plus you're paying buys you roughly ninety seconds in the air and nothing else. If you've already budgeted for the ferry and just want the thrill, that's a fair trade; if you're watching the number, the regular ferry gets you to the exact same metasequoia avenue.

Why does the price change before 8 a.m.?

Nami Island runs an early-bird rate: buy your ticket before 08:00 and it drops from ₩19,000 to ₩13,000, the same discount that applies again late in the day once the island's closer to closing. It's less a loyalty perk than an incentive to spread out the morning crowd.

🍂 Is late October the right week to go?

The island's ginkgo avenue, lined with roughly 2,000 trees, usually turns gold from late October into early-to-mid November, a few weeks after the metasequoia avenue starts going red. That metasequoia corridor is the same one from the 2002 drama Winter Sonata, planted back in 1997 from seedlings brought over from Seoul National University's agriculture college.

Why is the island named after a 15th-century general?

Nami Island takes its name from General Nam Yi, a Joseon-dynasty military commander executed in 1468 after a false treason accusation. Local legend held that his grave sat in a pile of stones on the island, but the real tomb is in Hwaseong, Gyeonggi Province; what's on the island is a cenotaph, a marker without a body.

A packed ferry queue on a clear October Saturday is the trade-off for that color, so if your schedule bends at all, a weekday morning crossing is the quieter version of the same walk. Nothing about the trees changes whether you show up at 9 a.m. or 1 p.m., only the size of the crowd standing in front of them.

My take I've been to Chuncheon a couple of times in the last two years, and I never once made it out to the actual island; Gapyeong's ferry dock was always on the way to somewhere else. If a day trip already feels like a lot of moving parts, I'd keep it simple: catch the 06:17 and treat the early-bird ticket as the whole plan, not just a discount. The zipwire is a fun add-on if your budget has room for it, but the ferry puts you on the same avenue for a third of the price, and the trees don't care how you got there.

FAQ
Is the zipwire open to everyone?
Not quite. Riders need to fall roughly between 140 and 200 cm tall and weigh between 35 and 120 kg, which rules out small children and anyone outside that window. Outside that range, the regular ferry still reaches the same avenue, just without the ninety seconds in the air.
Is there a direct bus that skips the train entirely?
Yes. A shuttle leaves Seoul each morning from Hongik University Station Exit 4, the Myeongdong parking lot, and Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station, arriving around 11:20 a.m. and returning from the island at 4:30 p.m.. It's the simpler option if you'd rather skip trains and taxis, though it locks you into a shorter window than setting your own pace with the ITX.

Go for the standard ferry ticket unless the zipwire is the actual point of your trip, and catch the early train out so you're walking the metasequoia avenue before the day-trip buses arrive. Buy before 8 a.m. if you can; it's the one line item on this page that changes based on when you show up.
