How to Make Kimchi Fried Rice (Kimchi-bokkeumbap) at Home

Kimchi hitting hot oil smells sharp and a little sweet before the rice ever goes in, the kind of smell that pulls people out of other rooms. It's the dish Korean kitchens reach for when the fridge looks empty but isn't, built from whatever's already sitting in a tub on the shelf. Nobody shops for it specially, and that's most of the appeal.

What trips people up isn't the cooking, which takes about fifteen minutes once the pan is hot. It's the kimchi itself: the wrong kind, at the wrong age, and the whole pan tastes like seasoned rice instead of the real thing.

What is kimchi-bokkeumbap (김치볶음밥)?

Kimchi fried rice: diced kimchi and cooked rice stir-fried together in the kimchi's own seasoning, usually with a cheap protein worked in and a fried egg on top. It's a budget staple in Korean homes, the dish people make when nothing else in the kitchen looks promising.

🍳 The recipe: kimchi fried rice for two

Two servings, 25 minutes end to end, rated 4.75 out of 5 by 75 home cooks. That's Korean Bapsang's bacon-and-kimchi version.

Ingredient Amount
Bacon, diced 3-4 strips (or 4 oz ham/Spam, or 1 can drained tuna)
Kimchi, diced ¾ cup
Onion, diced ¼ small onion
Scallion, chopped 1
Carrot, finely chopped (optional) 1 small
Kimchi juice 3-4 tbsp, if available
Soy sauce 1 tbsp
Gochujang 1 tbsp
Cooked rice 2½ cups
Sesame oil ½ tbsp
Salt and pepper to taste

Source: Korean Bapsang's kimchi fried rice recipe card, checked 2026-10-03. Optional toppings: sesame seeds, a fried egg, roasted seaweed strips.

Use kimchi that's actually fermented, not a fresh tub. The recipe's own author is direct about it: well-fermented kimchi gives the dish its "rich and robust flavor". A jar that's only a few days old hasn't built that sourness yet, and no amount of gochujang stirred in afterward makes up the difference.

  1. Cook the diced bacon in a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat until it's just starting to brown.

  2. Add the onion and scallion, stir-fry over high heat, then mix in the kimchi, optional carrot, kimchi juice, soy sauce and gochujang. Stir-fry 3-4 minutes until the kimchi softens and darkens.

  3. Add the rice and drop the heat to medium-low. Stir constantly for 3-4 minutes, breaking up any clumps until everything is evenly coated.

  4. Turn the heat back up to high and fry, stirring only occasionally, until the rice toasts a little at the edges. Season with salt and pepper, then mix in the sesame oil.

  5. Top with a fried egg and a scattering of sesame seeds or seaweed strips if using, and serve right away.

🌶️ Why does homemade kimchi fried rice taste flat?

Most flat batches come from the kimchi doing all the work alone, with nothing backing it up. Soy sauce and gochujang aren't garnish here; they're what carries the dish once the kimchi's own sourness runs out partway through the pan.

Gochugaru vs. gochujang: what's the difference?

Gochugaru is dry Korean chili flakes. Gochujang is a thick, fermented chili paste, sweetened not with sugar but with starch from glutinous rice that turns to sugar during fermentation. This recipe's base seasoning is gochujang, not gochugaru, so the two aren't interchangeable spoon for spoon.

Reach for more chili the wrong way and the dish turns salty, not spicier. The recipe's own note is specific: spoon in more gochujang for extra heat and the saltiness piles up fast, so gochugaru is the better way to turn up the spice without pushing the seasoning off balance.

Why is Spam such a big deal in Korea?

US troops brought it into Korea during the Korean War, and domestic production started in 1987. It's still a holiday gift today: boxed Spam sets are a go-to present for Chuseok and Seollal, and gift sets alone make up 60% of its annual sales in Korea.

None of the protein options need a separate shopping trip either. A half-used can of tuna or a few slices of deli ham left over from another meal drops straight in for the bacon, no change to the rest of the method.

  • What locals argue about Gochujang or gochugaru for extra spice. The recipe author comes down firmly on gochugaru, since more gochujang pushes the saltiness up faster than the heat.
  • Watch for The last two minutes on high heat. That's the step that toasts the rice instead of just warming it through, and it's also the easiest moment to walk away and come back to a scorched pan.
FAQ
Is kimchi fried rice vegan?
Not by default. A cured protein usually goes in, and separately, most jars lean on jeotgal, salted shrimp or fish sauce, for their depth. Swap in a plant-based jar and tofu for both, and the dish still comes together the same way.
My rice just finished cooking. Do I still need to do anything special with it?
Less than you'd expect. Microwaving a portion first is a fix for grains that have stiffened up in the refrigerator overnight, not a required move. A batch straight off the burner goes into the skillet as is, though a drier, day-old scoop still browns better at the end than one that's still warm and a little sticky.

The whole dish rests on one decision made before the pan ever heats up: whether the kimchi in the tub is actually ready to cook with. Taste a piece first, and if it's still mild, this is the one recipe where letting it sit a little longer pays off more than any amount of gochujang.
