Ganjang Gejang in Seoul: Where Locals Actually Eat It

Three Michelin-recognized soy-marinated crab restaurants in Gangnam, Mapo and Yeouido, each rated 4.2+ on Google Maps, with prices, hours and why one of them sells out by evening.

Quick answer
  • Three picks, three neighborhoods: Gebang Sikdang in Gangnam, Jinmi Sikdang in Mapo, Hwa Hae Dang in Yeouido, all rated 4.2 or better on Google Maps.

Lift the top shell off a ganjang gejang crab and the roe underneath is dark orange, almost lacquered, soaking in a marinade that's spent days turning soy sauce into something closer to crab stock than condiment. Koreans call it 밥도둑, the rice thief, because a spoonful of that sauce over warm rice is the whole reason the dish exists. It isn't a dish that photographs politely. Hands get messy, shells pile up at the edge of the plate, and the people at the next table are doing exactly the same thing without apologizing for it.

It's also had a quiet rise with visitors who don't grow up eating raw marinated seafood: a government tourism survey put it 6th among foreigners' favorite Korean dishes in 2022, climbing to 4th in 2023 and 3rd in 2024. If a plate of cold, raw crab sounds like a risk you'd rather not take on a short trip, the three places below are the ones locals and the Michelin Guide both point to.

What is gejang (게장)?

Raw crab cured in a marinade until it's safe and ready to eat without cooking. Ganjang gejang (간장게장) uses a soy-sauce-based brine; yangnyeom gejang (양념게장) swaps in a red chili paste instead, so the crab is spicier but the texture is similar. Both are eaten cold, straight from the shell.

🦀 Gebang Sikdang: the Gangnam counter that's made Michelin's list eight years straight

게방식당 (Gebang Sikdang), at 서울 강남구 선릉로131길 17, has been named to the Michelin Guide's Bib Gourmand list every year from 2018 through 2025. The exact subway station and walking time aren't confirmed, though the address sits near Seolleung Station on Line 2 and the Bundang Line.

It's open Monday to Saturday, 11:30-15:00 and 17:30-21:00, and closed on Sundays. The crab itself is priced by the day (시가, "market price") rather than posted as a fixed number, since the catch and the season both move it, so budget loosely and ask before you order.

Despite the plain name, Michelin describes a modern, cafeteria-style interior built for solo diners, with bar seating and both ganjang and yangnyeom (spicy) crab sets, plus crab-roe, abalone or shrimp rice bowls, each served with rice and soup alongside side dishes. It was started by a former fashion marketer whose parents had already run a gejang restaurant for 25 years, which is less a pivot than a return to the family business.

Order with "간장게장 세트 주세요" (gan-jang-ge-jang se-teu ju-se-yo, "soy-marinated crab set, please"), or ask for 양념게장 (yang-nyeom-ge-jang) if you want the spicy version. Takeout is available, and the restaurant takes cards. Whether an English menu exists is not confirmed.

What does siga (시가) mean on a Korean menu?

"Market price." It shows up next to seafood whose cost swings with the day's catch, like crab, abalone or live fish. There's no fixed number to quote, and a server will usually tell you the current price before you order if you ask.

🍚 Is Jinmi Sikdang worth booking ahead for?

Yes, if you call first: 진미식당 (Jinmi Sikdang) in Mapo sells only its ₩41,000 (about $30.78) ganjang gejang set, and it preps just enough crab for the day, so a late arrival can mean an empty case. The address is 서울 마포구 마포대로 186-6, about a 3-minute walk from Exit 4 of Aeogae Station.

It's open daily 12:00-20:00, with a break from 15:30 to 17:00, and closed Sundays. A 2021 Esquire Korea profile noted the restaurant had been selected to the Michelin Guide's Bib Gourmand list four years running as of that writing, using high-grade West Sea blue crab for its single dish.

Order with "게장정식 주세요" (ge-jang-jeong-sik ju-se-yo, "ganjang gejang set, please"). Reservations are recommended precisely because the kitchen doesn't hold back extra crab for walk-ins. The phone number is 02-3211-4468. Whether an English menu exists is not confirmed.

🌊 Hwa Hae Dang in Yeouido: the National Assembly crowd's lunch

화해당 여의도점 (Hwa Hae Dang, Yeouido branch) pairs ganjang gejang with hot stone-pot rice for ₩50,000 (about $37.54) per set, inside Gwangbok Hall at 서울 영등포구 국회대로62길 15, 1층 3호. The nearest subway station is likely National Assembly Station on Line 9, but the exact exit and walk time aren't confirmed.

It's open Tuesday to Saturday, 11:00-21:00, and closed Sundays and Mondays. The neighborhood is Seoul's political and financial district, so a weekday lunch here means sharing the room with National Assembly staffers and office workers rather than other tourists.

Order with "간장게장 돌솥밥 세트 주세요" (gan-jang-ge-jang dol-sot-bap se-teu ju-se-yo, "ganjang gejang stone-pot rice set, please"). Cards, phone number and an English menu are not confirmed.

My take I eat out around Gangnam and Samseong often, and visit Michelin-listed restaurants fairly often, so Gebang Sikdang's plain storefront is the kind of thing I'd normally walk past without a second look. That's exactly why I'd start there: it rewards a spontaneous dinner after work, no booking required, and the bar seating means showing up alone doesn't feel like a mistake. Jinmi is the one I'd actually plan around, since a single sold-out dish is a worse outcome than a crowded counter. Save it for a day when you can call ahead.
Go deeper
  • If you have one more hour Try both styles side by side if a shop offers it: ganjang gejang is milder and almost briny, yangnyeom gejang trades that for a chili kick, and Gebang Sikdang is the one restaurant here that sells both.
  • What locals argue about Whether the old pairing taboo has any teeth. The folk belief traces back centuries: Joseon king Gyeongjong is said by some historical records to have died after a meal of crab and persimmon together, though that story doesn't appear in every account of his death. A signed warning at a Seoul buffet revived the debate online in April 2026, centuries of folklore turning into a laminated sign.
  • Watch for Jinmi's single dish selling out. Since the kitchen only preps one day's portion of crab, a kitchen that's cleared out by evening is a Yelp complaint waiting to happen if you show up without calling first.
FAQ
Should an upset stomach the next day worry me?
One health journalist who dug into this concluded the real variable is storage and handling before your spoon ever touches the dish, not which fruit sat nearby on the table. Older diners or anyone with a sensitive gut should be choosier about where they sit down for this than a visitor who just wants to try it once.
Does an app booking beat showing up cold?
For one of the three, yes: Gebang's own listing names Naver as a reservation channel, so a quick tap in that app secures a seat without a Korean phone call. Neither of the other two advertises that option, so plan on dialing in or having your hotel's front desk make the call.

Pick Gebang Sikdang for a no-reservation dinner in Gangnam, and call ahead for Jinmi if Mapo is more your route.
