MAMA is in Osaka, Golden Disc already left Korea, and Seoul Music Awards sold out in 14 minutes. Here's the one big show still happening in Seoul.

A little after 8 p.m. on a Monday in October, a few hundred thousand phones in Korea were all staring at the same gray button on the Melon app, waiting for it to turn clickable. That's what a presale for a K-pop awards show looks like now: no red carpet yet, no stage, just a countdown and a seat map that fills in while you watch. The show on the other end of that countdown, MMA2026, is the rare one of its kind that still happens where most of its fans actually live.

What is a "dome" venue (돔구장)?

A retractable- or fixed-roof stadium built for both baseball and large indoor concerts. Korea has only a handful, which is part of why booking one for a two-night awards show is such a scramble.

🎫 Which K-pop awards show is actually in Korea this year?

Only one of the big four: MMA2026, November 14 and 15 at Gocheok Sky Dome, in Guro District in western Seoul. It's the Melon Music Awards' 18th edition and the first time it's run over two days, splitting 26 performing acts across the two nights.

Everything else on a fan's annual calendar has either left Korea or already come and gone. The 2026 MAMA AWARDS run November 20-21 at Kyocera Dome in Osaka, Japan, with Visa as title sponsor for a third straight year. The 40th Golden Disc Awards already happened, on January 10, at Taipei Dome in Taiwan, where all 35,000 seats sold out the moment tickets opened; the 41st edition moves again, to an outdoor stage in Hanoi, Vietnam, for January 9-10, 2027. The 35th Seoul Music Awards also already happened, on June 20 at Inspire Arena on Incheon's Yeongjong Island, and its tickets sold out in 14 minutes on NOL Ticket.

✈️ Why do MAMA and Golden Disc keep leaving Korea?

Mostly money, and Korea doesn't have many venues big enough to compete anyway. An industry insider told the Korea Times it "ultimately comes down to money": overseas investment can cover venue rental, artist fees, and production costs outright, and strong local demand means a crowd abroad is more likely to pay VIP prices Korea's own capped tickets can't charge. In a December 2024 report, those overseas VIP seats ran ₩300,000 (about $250 at the time) to ₩400,000. The same insider said organizers "actively consider" a Korean venue every year, but called the logistics "an uphill battle".

The venue shortage is real, not an excuse. Jamsil Olympic Stadium has been under renovation and off-limits for shows, and Seoul World Cup Stadium's turf isn't built for concert crowds, since sports fans and athletes have complained that concert crowds damage the field, leaving organizers to book suburban arenas like Goyang Sports Complex or the Incheon dome instead of anything inside Seoul proper. Golden Disc's January ceremony was its seventh time overseas since it first left Korea for Japan in 2012.

🎟️ How do you actually get in, or watch from home?

Two presale rounds through Melon, both already run, and a general sale hasn't been confirmed yet. The first round, open only to Melon members on the GOLD tier or higher with at least a year's subscription, ran four hours on Monday, October 19, from 8 p.m. to 11:59 p.m., covering both floor and tiered seats. The second round opened to any paying Melon member on October 22 at 8 p.m., covering tiered seats plus whatever floor seats got cancelled out of round one. Either way, the limit is two tickets per Melon ID across both rounds combined.

What is Melon's membership tier system (GOLD/VIP/MVIP)?

Melon ranks paying subscribers by how long and how much they've used the service. Higher tiers get earlier access to presales like this one; a brand-new Melon account won't see round one at all.

So what's left for anyone reading this after the fact? Watch ticket.melon.com for a possible third round or late cancellations, since Kakao hasn't said whether general sale is coming at all before the show. Create and verify a paid Melon membership now even if round one has passed; round two's window may still be open depending on when this reaches you, and any future round will run the same way.

MAMA works differently and skips Melon entirely. Its first access point was a Visa Presale, open to Visa cardholders worldwide from October 13 to 18, with results visible on the ticketing partner Qoo10 from October 21. That window has already closed by the time most readers see this; MAMA's organizer, CJ ENM, hasn't announced a general sale date.

If a ticket doesn't come through, there's still a way to watch, just not always for free and not always confirmed yet. MAMA streams live worldwide through Mnet Plus both nights, and its new MAMA FAN FESTA runs offline in Osaka and online on the same platform from November 9 through 21, bundled into the Visa Presale registration as a "Super Pass". The Golden Disc Awards broadcast live on Naver Chzzk for viewers in Korea and on the platform Prism internationally. MMA2026 hasn't named an international stream yet; Kakao Entertainment's own announcement covers dates, venue, and theme but says nothing about broadcast partners.

My take Chasing one of these shows abroad is a real expense on top of the ticket itself, and it's a fair thing to resent if you grew up watching these ceremonies on Korean TV at home. If I were picking just one shot this year, I'd put it on MMA2026, since it's the single show that doesn't also ask for a flight. Everything else is either already sold out or a trip you'd need to plan and budget for separately, well before a ticket is even guaranteed.

FAQ
Is the Seoul Music Awards really gone for this year?
No path back in for now. Sports Seoul, the outlet behind the ceremony, hasn't named a resale option for anyone who missed that June sale, and nothing about a follow-up date has leaked; bookmark the organizing committee's own notices rather than trusting secondhand chatter.
Can I resell my MMA2026 ticket if I can't go anymore?
At face value, yes; above face value, you're risking the same penalty concert scalpers face. A decree in force since August 28, 2026 fines illegal resale 2 to 50 times the sale amount once it hits ₩50,000 and two or more tickets for any ticketed performance, awards shows included.

None of this year's big four is a sure thing, and three of the four are either abroad or already over. My next check on this is whenever Kakao confirms whether MMA2026 gets a general ticket sale, since that's the one window still open for anyone reading this before November 14.
