Seoul Silver Grass Festival 2026: Haneul Park Guide

Haneul Park's silver grass festival runs Oct 17-23, 2026, open till 21:00. Line 6, the ₩2,000 electric car or the stairs, and what an evening costs.

Quick answer
  • One week of late nights: the 25th Seoul Silver Grass Festival fills Haneul Park from Oct 17 to 23, 2026, 10:00 to 21:00 every day.
  • The hill is the only real cost: walk up for free or ride the ₩2,000 electric car, then the grass, the views and the paths are yours.
  • Getting there: leave the car at home and take Line 6 to World Cup Stadium Station, because the city itself warns that traffic gets very heavy.

Sixty thousand pyeong of silver grass now grows on a hill that Seoul once filled with its own trash. In daylight the wind pushes the plumes into slow waves, and after dark last year's festival lit the paths with LED lights and media art. Up there the city drops away into a long horizon line, with the Han River on one side and nothing taller than you but the sky. If Seoul has been all subway crowds and shop signs so far, this is the evening it goes quiet and wide around you.

What is eoksae (억새)?

Korean silver grass: a tall autumn grass whose feathery flower heads turn silver in October. People often mix it up with galdae (갈대, reeds); Seoul's own festival writers point out that the silver sheen of the flowers is what sets eoksae apart.

📅 When is the 2026 Seoul Silver Grass Festival?

The 25th Seoul Silver Grass Festival runs Saturday, Oct 17 to Friday, Oct 23, 2026, from 10:00 to 21:00 daily, across Haneul Park in World Cup Park. That's straight from Seoul's official festival notice, updated Sep 28, 2026, which gives this year's theme as 억새, 자연이 건네는 위로, roughly "silver grass, the comfort nature offers". Questions go to the city's Western Parks Leisure Center at 02-300-5574.

Those hours matter more than they look. Last year the city kept World Cup Park open only until 19:00 in October, stretched it to 21:00 for festival week, then held the grass fields open until 20:00 through Nov 2. Seoul hasn't posted 2026's after-festival hours yet, so treat the 21:00 close as a festival-week privilege.

What did last year's edition actually hold? The 2025 program ran busking from 14:00 to 18:00, media art from 19:00, and 16 photo zones scattered through the grass. A stamp tour handed out festival goods to anyone who collected 5 stamps at the main photo zones. The festival itself has run since World Cup Park opened in 2002.

What was Nanjido (난지도)?

A Han River island that Seoul used as its garbage landfill. It was rebuilt as World Cup Park, and Haneul Park, one of its five parks, opened in 2002 to mark the 17th FIFA World Cup. Because it's now an ecological park, it closes at night to protect the species living there.

🚇 How do you get to Haneul Park, and up the hill?

Take Line 6 to World Cup Stadium Station, leave by Exit 1, then either ride the paid Maengkkongi electric car or climb on foot. The festival notice says the walk from World Cup Stadium Station or Mapo-gu Office Station takes about 40 minutes, and lists the electric car from the Nanjicheon Park parking lot as the alternative. Exit 1 to the electric car ticket booth is 803 m on Visit Seoul's map.

The subway fare is the easy part. An adult T-money tap costs ₩1,550 for the first 10 km, with ₩100 added for each 5 km after that; a single-journey paper ticket is ₩1,650. If you're staying around Hongdae, transfer to Line 6 at Hapjeong, and World Cup Stadium is three stops on.

What is the Maengkkongi electric car (맹꽁이 전기차)?

A paid electric shuttle that runs between Nanjicheon Park and the top of Haneul Park. It's the park's own ride, not a city bus, and it has its own ticket booth near the bottom of the hill.

Picture arriving at the foot of the hill on a festival Saturday: one way up has a ticket booth and a line, the other is a long flight of stairs. Think of the electric car as a ₩2,000 elevator. Visit Seoul lists 2026 fares of ₩2,000 one way and ₩3,000 round trip for adults, and ₩1,500 one way or ₩2,200 round trip for children aged 4 to 12. For a couple riding both ways that's ₩6,000, a little more than one ₩4,700 coffee, to save your legs for the grass.

The free options are a steep staircase or a gentler loop trail; a Seoul citizen reporter counted 331 steps on the stairs last year. One of those reports noted the electric car line ran long during festival week. My pick: the stairs up while there's light for the view, then the gentle trail back down.

For timing the trip home, Line 6 runs well past the park's closing time. A timetable listing for World Cup Stadium Station shows the first train toward Gongdeok at 05:35, the last at 00:45 on weekdays and 23:49 on Sundays and holidays. The 21:00 gate sets your evening, not the subway.

💸 What does an evening at the silver grass festival cost?

Plan on roughly ₩15,000 a person for the whole evening, and most of that is food you'd buy anyway. Last year's festival listed entry as free, so the bill is transit, the hill and a snack.

Ride a farther line into Line 6 and the fare climbs by ₩100 per extra 5 km, so budget a little more for a longer ride.

My take A festival in a city you don't know can feel like a gamble: is it a crowd with a few plants, or is it actually worth an evening? This one earns it, because the grass is the show and it doesn't need a stage. Honestly? I'd go on a weekday, climb the stairs in late afternoon, and stay until the lights come on. If a weekend is your only chance, skip the electric car queue and take the stairs; the line was the slow part last year.

FAQ
Can I bring my dog?
Yes, on the grass paths. Visit Seoul's 2026 notes allow dogs everywhere in the park except the children's playgrounds, the forest play area, the camping ground, the park golf course and the Nanjicheon stream. There's also a free dog play area in the Pyeonghwa Park parking lot, block G, for registered dogs with an owner aged 13 or older.
Can I stay up there for night photos after 21:00?
No. Access is controlled overnight so that species sheltering on the restored landfill aren't disturbed, which means a tripod shot has to happen before 21:00. Once festival week ends, opening times move month by month with sunset; ring the park office on 02-300-5501 or the 1330 tourist hotline before heading out.

Go once during festival week, on a weekday if you can, and give yourself the hour before dark. The grass will still be there the following week, but the 21:00 gate and the lights may not be. Check the city's notice page again around Oct 16 for the 2026 program and any after-festival hours.
